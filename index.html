A SMALL FIELD GUIDE

There is no wrong weather.

  1. 01
    Choose a medium. Make it your color.

    Cloud forms molten color, Rain leaves liquid streaks, Wind stirs the currents, and Light brings a soft glow.

  2. 02
    Hold your mouse and draw.

    Your speed and direction shape each mark. Try a slow curve, a quick sweep, or layers of different colors.

  3. 03
    Let it drift. Pause whenever you like.

    Your colors keep moving after your gesture ends. When a moment feels right, choose Make picture to save it.
